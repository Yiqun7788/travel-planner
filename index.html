Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Information We Collect

When you use Wise Itinerary, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information — your email address when you sign in
  • Trip planning data — destinations, dates, budget, number of travelers, transportation preferences, interests, and any additional instructions you provide
  • Contact form data — your email address and message when you use the Contact form

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized travel itineraries
  • To process payments for premium credits
  • To save and sync your trips across devices (when signed in)

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Wise Itinerary:

  • Supabase — authentication and trip data storage
  • Stripe — payment processing. Your card details are handled entirely by Stripe and are never stored on our servers.
  • Google Gemini — used alongside our automated planning logic to generate itineraries. Trip details you provide (destination, dates, preferences) are sent to Google Gemini to help create your itinerary.
  • Komoot Photon — location autocomplete for destination search
  • Web3Forms — contact form delivery. When you submit the Contact form, your email address and message are sent through Web3Forms to reach us.

Local Storage

Trip data may be stored in your browser's sessionStorage or localStorage for convenience, so you can access your itinerary without being signed in. This data stays on your device.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

No Tracking

We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or tracking pixels. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Contact form on our homepage.
